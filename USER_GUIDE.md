## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Visit https://makefarmhub.vercel.app
2. Click **Sign Up** in the top right
3. Choose your role:
   - **Farmer**: Sell crops, livestock, or farm products
   - **Buyer**: Purchase agricultural products
   - **Transporter**: Offer delivery services
4. Enter your details:
   - Full name
   - Phone number (Zimbabwe format: +263...)
   - Email address
   - Password
5. Verify your email and phone number

### Verifying Your Account

**Email Verification:**
1. Check your email inbox
2. Click the verification link
3. Your account is now verified

**Phone Verification:**
1. You'll receive a 6-digit OTP via SMS
2. Enter the code in the verification page
3. Code expires in 10 minutes

### Enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

For extra security:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Security**
2. Click **Enable 2FA**
3. Scan the QR code with Google Authenticator or Authy
4. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm
5. **Save your backup codes** in a safe place

## For Farmers

### Creating a Listing

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Create Listing**
2. Choose category:
   - Crops (Maize, Wheat, Vegetables, etc.)
   - Livestock (Cattle, Goats, Chickens)
   - Farm Equipment
   - Seeds & Fertilizers
3. Fill in details:
   - **Title**: Clear, descriptive name
   - **Description**: Quality, quantity, condition
   - **Price**: Per unit (kg, bag, head, etc.)
   - **Quantity Available**
   - **Location**
   - **Delivery Options**
4. Upload photos (up to 5):
   - Clear, well-lit images
   - Show product from multiple angles
   - Include close-ups of quality
5. Click **Publish Listing**

### Managing Orders

**When You Receive an Order:**
1. You'll get a notification
2. Review order details
3. Click **Confirm Order** or **Reject**
4. If confirmed:
   - Prepare the product
   - Update order status
   - Arrange delivery/pickup
5. Once delivered, click **Mark as Delivered**
6. Funds are released to your wallet after buyer confirms

### Using the Weather Dashboard

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Weather**
2. View:
   - Current temperature and conditions
   - 7-day forecast
   - Rainfall predictions
3. Get farming recommendations based on weather
4. Check planting calendar for your crops

### Market Price Tracker

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Market Prices**
2. View current prices for major commodities
3. See price trends (up/down/stable)
4. Set price alerts:
   - Choose commodity
   - Set target price
   - Get notified when price reached

## For Buyers

### Finding Products

**Search:**
1. Use search bar at top
2. Enter product name (e.g., "maize")
3. Use voice search icon 🎤 to speak your query

**Filters:**
- Category
- Price range
- Location
- Seller rating
- Delivery options

### Placing an Order

1. Click on product listing
2. Review:
   - Product details
   - Seller information
   - Reviews and ratings
3. Select quantity
4. Click **Buy Now** or **Add to Cart**
5. Choose payment method:
   - Card (Stripe)
   - Mobile Money (EcoCash, OneMoney, etc.)
   - Wallet Balance
6. Confirm order
7. Track delivery status

### Using Wishlist

1. Click the ❤️ icon on any product
2. Access wishlist from top menu
3. View all saved items
4. Get notified of price drops
5. Quick purchase from wishlist

### Writing Reviews

After receiving your order:
1. Go to **Orders** → Select completed order
2. Click **Write Review**
3. Rate seller (1-5 stars)
4. Rate product quality
5. Write detailed feedback
6. Submit review

1. Browse available transport requests

   - Cargo type

4. Contact buyer/seller
5. Arrange pickup details
6. Update status during delivery
7. Get paid after successful delivery

## Payments & Wallet

### Depositing Funds

**Via Card (Stripe):**
1. Go to **Wallet** → **Deposit**
2. Enter amount
3. Click **Pay with Card**
4. Enter card details
5. Confirm payment
6. Funds appear instantly

**Via Mobile Money:**
1. Go to **Wallet** → **Deposit**
2. Choose provider (EcoCash, OneMoney, etc.)
3. Enter amount and phone number
4. Follow USSD prompts on your phone
5. Approve payment
6. Funds credited within minutes

### Withdrawing Funds

1. Go to **Wallet** → **Withdraw**
2. Enter amount (minimum $10)
3. Choose withdrawal method:
   - Bank transfer
   - Mobile money
4. Enter destination details
5. Confirm withdrawal
6. Processing time: 1-3 business days

### Transaction History

- View all transactions in **Wallet** → **Transactions**
- Filter by type (deposit, withdrawal, payment, refund)
- Export to CSV for records
- Download monthly statements

## Mobile Money Guide

### Supported Providers

- **EcoCash** (Econet): 077/078
- **OneMoney** (NetOne): 071
- **InnBucks** (All networks)
- **Telecash** (Telecel): 073

### How to Pay with EcoCash

1. Select EcoCash as payment method
2. Enter your Econet number
3. System sends payment request
4. On your phone:
   - Dial *151#
   - Go to "Send Money"
   - Select "To EcoCash User"
   - Enter merchant number
   - Enter amount
   - Enter PIN
   - Confirm
5. Payment confirmed automatically

### How to Pay with OneMoney

1. Select OneMoney as payment method
2. Enter your NetOne number
3. Follow USSD prompts:
   - Dial *111#
   - Select "Send Money"
   - Select "To OneMoney"
   - Enter details
   - Enter PIN
4. Confirm payment

### Troubleshooting Mobile Money

**Payment Not Going Through:**
- Check phone number is correct
- Ensure sufficient balance
- Verify PIN is correct
- Check network connectivity

**Payment Pending:**
- Wait 5-10 minutes for processing
- Check phone for USSD prompt
- Contact support if issue persists

## Security Features

### Protecting Your Account

1. **Use Strong Password:**
   - Minimum 8 characters
   - Mix of letters, numbers, symbols
   - Don't reuse passwords

2. **Enable 2FA:**
   - Adds extra layer of security
   - Required for high-value transactions

3. **Verify Sellers/Buyers:**
   - Check ratings and reviews
   - Look for verified badge ✓
   - Read feedback from others

### Secure Transactions

- All payments use escrow system
- Funds held until delivery confirmed
- Dispute resolution available
- Full refund if seller doesn't deliver

### Reporting Suspicious Activity

1. Click report button on listing/user
2. Select reason:
   - Fraud/Scam
   - Fake listing
   - Harassment
   - Other
3. Provide details
4. Admin reviews within 24 hours

## Tips & Best Practices
